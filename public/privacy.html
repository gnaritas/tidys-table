Privacy, plainly

The short version: we don't track you

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics and no advertising scripts. Nothing is stored in your browser by us.

The contact form: the name, phone, email, and message you send are passed directly to Tidy's Table so the shop can answer you. They aren't added to any marketing list and aren't sold or shared with anyone else.

The map on this site is embedded from Google Maps. When you view it, Google receives standard technical information (your IP address among it) the same as it would on any site that embeds a map.

Server basics: like any website, the server keeps brief technical logs (requested page, approximate time, browser type) used to keep the site running.

Questions or want your message deleted? Call (570) 261-4180 or write to tidystable@gmail.com and say the word.

This page covers the pages on this site only, not the Facebook page or Google Maps themselves.
