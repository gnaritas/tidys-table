A year in the making

The old pizza building on the boulevard, completely reborn

Tidy's Table is named for a 170-pound Great Dane named Titan who always seemed to be sitting at the table with you.

Two Mountain Top owners — Vicky Page of Mountain Top Kids and her business partner Kim Rosania, whose family once ran Ma's Family Restaurant here — took over the old Januzzi's Pizza building on N Mountain Blvd, stripped it to the studs, and rebuilt it as a bright breakfast and lunch dining room. The dog on the sign is Tidy.
