Why we do breakfast

Restaurant people, raised on it

Tidy's Table runs on restaurant genes. Partner Kim Rosania's family once operated Ma's Family Restaurant right here in Mountain Top, and co-owner Vicky Page has spent years running Mountain Top Kids across town — this pair knows what a good morning looks like for the people who walk in.

When the two of them decided what Mountain Top still needed, the answer was simple: a proper breakfast and lunch spot — so that's exactly what this is, and nothing else.

How breakfast works here

The classics, with our take on them

"A fresh take on traditional offerings" — that's the promise on our sign, and it's how the morning kitchen runs.

Eggs fixed however you like them, the griddle running all morning, and plates that taste like somebody's family kitchen — because it sort of is. The current menu changes as we add and retire items on guests' advice, so the newest specials are posted on our Facebook page first.
