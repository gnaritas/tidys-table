Our story

Named for the dog who sat at the table

Vicky Page had a Great Dane named Titan — 170 pounds of charm who greeted everyone at the door and ate with his people because he was too big not to. His nickname was Tidy. When it came time to name a welcoming breakfast spot, there was only ever one answer.

The building

From square one, on purpose

A little over a year ago, Vicky Page bought the old Januzzi's Pizza building on N Mountain Boulevard. She and her business partner Kim Rosania didn't mean to open a restaurant. A restaurant is what Mountain Top needed.

"We basically had to start out from scratch, from square one," Page said of the renovation — new everything, from kitchen systems to dining room, so that anyone walking in would recognize it for what it is: a new building with new life in it.

Kim grew up around this business — her family once ran Ma's Family Restaurant here in Mountain Top. Between the two of them, the recipe was simple: do breakfast and lunch properly, the way this town eats them.

Why we do it

Neighbors first

"We want to grow in our corner, not drive other businesses out."
— from the owners' first-week letter to Mountain Top

Breakfast & lunch only

A real breakfast-and-lunch gap on the boulevard — that was the need the owners set out to fill, so they fill it and stop at three.

We listen

Opening week came with questionnaires from guests. The owners read every one and rebuilt parts of the menu on the advice inside them.

Early reviews: welcome accepted

Soft-opening weekends ran packed from open to close, and early guests on Facebook rate the table 100% recommended. The grand opening, Tuesday October 20, 2026, was announced with a promise the owners still stand behind: no reservations needed.

Come by

Seven days a week, 6am to 3pm

Tidy would have liked that — the doors open early, the coffee on, everybody welcome at the table.
