Why stop at three

No dinner here — and that's the whole idea

We could stay open for dinner. We won't. The owners picked breakfast and lunch on purpose: it's what this kitchen does best, and it leaves the evening tables to the restaurants around Mountain Top that do dinners better than anyone.

It's the same thinking behind the menu — join the community, grow in your own corner, let the neighbors keep their specialties. Bagels, for one: there's a great bagel spot down the road, and we send people to it.
