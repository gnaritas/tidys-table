Questions guests ask most

No reservations. No dinner. No problem.

Do I need a reservation?

No. Since the grand opening on October 20, 2026, it's been walk-in only — come when you like. Weekends get busy from open to close, so early birds get the quiet corners.

What are your hours?

6am to 3pm, every single day of the week. Breakfast runs all morning and lunch runs alongside it until we close.

Do you serve dinner?

Never — by design. We do breakfast and lunch and do them right, and leave the evening tables to the many good dinner restaurants around Mountain Top.

Do you have bagels?

No bagels here, also on purpose — there's a beloved bagel neighbor nearby and we'd rather send you to them than step on their toes. We'll bring the coffee.

Where did the name (and the dog on the sign) come from?

Owner Vicky Page's Great Dane, Titan — 170 pounds, friends with everyone, always at the table. His nickname was Tidy. Read the full story.

Is the menu posted anywhere?

Menus evolve as the kitchen listens to guests — which is exactly what's happening right now. Newest items and specials are posted on our Facebook page, or call and ask what's running today: (570) 261-4180.
